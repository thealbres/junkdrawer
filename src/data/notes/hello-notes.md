This is the first note. Notes live in `src/data/notes/` and show up at `/notes`, separate from the longer-form posts in `/posts`.

To add a new one, drop a markdown file here with at least `title`, `description`, and `pubDatetime` in the frontmatter — same rules as posts (see [Adding new posts](/posts/adding-new-posts-in-astropaper-theme)).
